Stratum-1 Precision Hardware Clock

Technologies: Verilog, Lattice iCE40UP5K FPGA (iCEBreaker), NEO-6M GPS, UART, Digital Logic

Designed and implemented a Stratum-1 precision hardware clock synchronizer and GPS pass-through bridge using pure digital logic on a Lattice iCE40UP5K FPGA (iCEBreaker) and a NEO-6M GPS module.

Unlike traditional embedded systems, this project completely bypasses the need for microprocessors or software operating systems, handling all GPS data extraction and synchronization at the speed of light in custom silicon.

  • Custom Verilog UART: Developed a custom hardware UART receiver in Verilog to sample 9600 baud serial data directly from the GPS module.
  • State Machine Parsing: Engineered a robust state machine to parse the live NMEA data stream, isolate the $GPRMC atomic time sentences, and pulse a hardware LED in perfect sync with the satellite clocks.
  • Routing & Constraints: Overcame complex PCF (Physical Constraint File) compiler routing challenges and toolchain bugs by remapping internal oscillator clocks and I/O blocks.
  • Pass-Through Bridge: Utilized FPGA parallel processing to simultaneously route a physical pass-through bridge to the FTDI USB port, allowing live visualization of raw satellite tracking data in the u-blox u-center software dashboard.

Wi-Fi Router UPS Power Bank

Technologies: BMS Integration, DC-DC Converters, 3S Lithium-ion, Circuit Calibration

A custom-built DC Uninterruptible Power Supply (UPS) designed to seamlessly provide reliable backup power for a Wi-Fi router during grid outages.

  • Robust Interface: Housed in a clean, custom white enclosure with a textured black faceplate. The control panel includes a 3-digit 7-segment display for real-time voltage monitoring, a rotary knob for fine voltage adjustment, dual 5.5mm DC jacks, and a main toggle switch.
  • Power Management: Powered by a 3S 12.6V lithium-ion battery pack with a dedicated Battery Management System (BMS) for safe charging, discharging, and cell balancing. A DC-DC converter module with onboard inductors and capacitors regulates a highly stable output voltage.

VAF-M2-2280-Ti135 — 8-Layer HDI PCIe FPGA Compute Accelerator

Technologies: 8-Layer HDI PCB, Efinix Titanium Ti135N484I4 (484-BGA), Winbond 512 MiB LPDDR4X (200-BGA), PCIe Gen2, Controlled Impedance, KiCad 10

Overview

Engineered a compact M.2 2280 (Key M, 22 × 80 mm) PCIe Gen2 FPGA compute accelerator pairing an Efinix Titanium Ti135N484I4 FPGA (484-ball BGA, 0.8 mm pitch) with Winbond W66CP6RBHAFJ 512 MiB 16-bit LPDDR4X SDRAM (200-ball VFBGA).

Key Engineering Highlights

  • 8-Layer Symmetrical HDI Stackup (0.80 mm): Solid ground reference planes shielding internal symmetrical stripline layers (In2.Signal & In5.Signal) tuned for strict controlled impedance (Z0 = 40.0 Ω, Zdiff = 80.0 Ω).
