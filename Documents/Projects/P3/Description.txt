Excited to share my project: a Power Regulator and LED Board designed in Altium Designer! 🚀
As a 5th-semester EEE student, taking this from schematic to a fully routed board has been an incredible hands-on learning experience.
How it works: The board is designed to take an unregulated input voltage and step it down to a clean, stable DC output. The layout incorporates filtering capacitors and a regulator IC to ensure steady power delivery to safely drive the onboard LED indicators.
A major focus of this build was implementing top and bottom layer Polygon Pours (Copper Pours) to create robust Ground (GND) planes. This was a crucial step for:
✅ Thermal Management: Acting as a heatsink for the power regulator. 
✅ Signal Integrity: Providing a low-impedance return path. 
✅ Noise Reduction: Shielding the circuit from EMI.
Looking forward to bringing these hardware design skills into more complex IoT and embedded system projects! 💡
