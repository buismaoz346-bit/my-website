Project Overview
A custom-built DC power bank / uninterruptible power supply (UPS) designed to provide reliable backup power for a Wi-Fi router during outages.

Hardware & Components
Enclosure: Custom white plastic housing with a textured black faceplate.

Battery System: A lithium-ion battery pack equipped with an integrated Battery Management System (BMS) board to ensure safe charging, discharging, and cell balancing (likely a 3S 12.6V configuration).

Power Regulation: A DC-DC step-down/step-up converter module featuring onboard inductors, capacitors, and a potentiometer to regulate a stable output voltage regardless of the battery's charge level.

Interface & I/O:

Digital 3-digit 7-segment display for real-time voltage monitoring.

Rotary knob for voltage adjustment.

Dual DC barrel jacks (standard 5.5mm) for charging input and power output.

Main power toggle switch.

Protection Circuitry: A panel-mounted fuse holder protects against overcurrent, and an inline diode is wired in series with the output to prevent reverse polarity or backfeeding.

Testing & Calibration
The internal wiring routes the battery through the BMS, switch, and DC-DC converter before reaching the output jack. During testing, a digital multimeter (VC9205A) was used to probe the output line (post-diode) and calibrate the system to exactly 13.0V. This slightly elevated voltage likely compensates for the forward voltage drop of the inline diode, ensuring a clean 12V reaches the router under load.
