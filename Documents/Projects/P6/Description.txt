The IncliSense project is a precision digital leveling device built to provide high-accuracy numerical feedback, improving upon traditional mechanical levels. Driven by an Arduino Nano and an MPU6050 accelerometer/gyroscope, the system processes dual-axis spatial data to trigger a responsive interface of 11 LEDs and an integrated buzzer. Housed in a custom 3D-printed ABS enclosure, IncliSense also features PC telemetry for IoT connectivity, making it a robust, industrial-grade engineering prototype.
